# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-21

Synapse Health Sync is a personal-use application. It is used only by its
developer, with the developer's own Google account. It is not offered to other
users.

## Data accessed

The application reads the account owner's own health data through the Google
Health API: sleep, heart rate, weight, steps and distance.

## How the data is used

The data is retrieved on the owner's own computer and used only for the owner's
personal health review. The retrieved values may be shown to an AI assistant
(Claude, by Anthropic) within the owner's own private sessions, so that it can
answer the owner's questions. The data is not used for advertising, is not sold,
and is not shared with any other third party.

## Storage

OAuth credentials are stored only on the owner's local computer and are not
published. Health data is not stored on any server operated by the developer.

## Contact

For questions, open an issue in this repository.
